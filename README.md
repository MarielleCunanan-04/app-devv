---

## Movie Summary
*Cinderella* tells the story of **Ella**, a kind-hearted young woman who dreams of creating her own fashion designs.  
With the help of her **Fab G** (Fairy Godmother), she gets the chance to attend the royal ball — not to find love, but to chase her dream of independence and success.

---

## Main Characters
- **Cinderella (Camila Cabello)** – A brave and creative young woman who dreams of becoming a fashion designer  
- **Prince Robert (Nicholas Galitzine)** – A charming prince who values freedom and love over royal duty  
- **Fab G (Billy Porter)** – The magical godparent who helps Ella attend the ball  
- **Queen Beatrice (Minnie Driver)** – The prince’s supportive mother  
- **King Rowan (Pierce Brosnan)** – The traditional ruler who expects his son to marry for power  
- **Vivian (Idina Menzel)** – Cinderella’s stepmother who struggles between jealousy and protection  
- **Stepsisters (Charlotte Spencer, Maddie Baillio)** – Comically mean but not entirely heartless  

---

## Key Scenes
1. **“Dreams Don’t Come True”** – Cinderella expresses her wish for freedom  
2. **“The Dress Transformation”** – Fab G creates her magical gown  
3. **“The Royal Ball”** – Cinderella stuns everyone with her confidence  
4. **“Midnight Escape”** – The magic fades as the clock strikes twelve  
5. **“Follow Your Dreams”** – Cinderella chooses her career over royalty
